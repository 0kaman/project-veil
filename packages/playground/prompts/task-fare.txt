Find the cheapest NONSTOP flight from Bangalore (BLR) to Delhi (DEL) departing on
12 August 2026. Tell me the airline, the departure time, and the fare in rupees.

This is a real task, not a script. Nobody is going to tell you which tools to use or
in what order - work that out yourself. Use the cheapest approach that can actually
answer the question, and reach for something heavier only when the cheaper thing has
demonstrably failed.

Rules:
- Do not book anything. Do not enter a name, email address, phone number, or any
  payment detail. Do not sign in and do not create an account. You are finding a
  price, not buying a ticket.
- If a site blocks you, say so plainly, name what blocked you, and go to a different
  source rather than fighting it.
- Do not invent or estimate a number. If you cannot get a real fare for that exact
  date, say you could not, and say precisely where you got stuck.

If you find PART of the answer, keep it. Say what you have and what is still
missing, rather than discarding it and starting again somewhere else. A fare for
the right date with no airline attached is worth more than nothing, and you should
report it as exactly that. Do not abandon a partial result silently.

When you have an answer - or have concluded you cannot get one - stop and report:

- The fare, the airline, and the departure time, with the URL you took it from.
- Whether that number is for the exact date asked, or a generic "fares from" figure
  you found instead. Be explicit about which one it is.
- Which path actually answered it: search alone, reading a page, or driving a
  browser. If you escalated, say what specifically forced the escalation - not "the
  page needed JavaScript" in the abstract, but what you saw that told you so.
- Anything in the toolchain that got in your way, in your own words. Be blunt. If a
  receipt told you something that turned out to be wrong or useless, say so.
