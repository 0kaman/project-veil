Fusion milestone reached

Researchers reported net energy gain from a fusion reaction for the second time this year, confirming the earlier result was not a fluke. The experiment used one hundred and ninety two lasers focused on a fuel pellet smaller than a peppercorn, producing more energy than the lasers delivered.

The team cautioned that the accounting excludes the enormous energy needed to power the lasers themselves, so a commercial reactor remains distant. Still, crossing the scientific break even line twice moves the field from possibility to engineering problem.
